Tab Commander Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2, 2026

Tab Commander is a Chrome extension by Edwin Murillo. This policy explains what it does with data.

What the extension reads

To show your dashboard, it reads the titles, URLs and icons of your open tabs and which window they belong to.

Where it is stored

This tab list is saved only in your browser's local extension storage on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Removing the extension removes it.

Voice commands

If you click the microphone, the extension uses Chrome's built-in Web Speech API. Chrome may process the audio through Google's speech service under Google's privacy policy. Tab Commander does not record, store, or send audio itself.

What we do not do

  • No servers, accounts, analytics, tracking or ads
  • No selling or sharing of data
  • No remote code
